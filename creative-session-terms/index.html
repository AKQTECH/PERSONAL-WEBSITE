Creative Session Terms

Terms governing your paid Creative Session with April Korto Quioh and Palava Productions.

Please Review Before Booking

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Creative Session Terms - □ x

These Creative Session Terms govern the paid creative session booked with Palava Productions and April Korto Quioh, collectively referred to as “Palava.” The person or entity purchasing, booking, or attending the session is referred to as “Client.”

1. The Creative Session

The Teaser is a live, ninety-minute creative presentation, strategic discussion, and writers’ room focused on Client’s potential use of serialized vertical storytelling.

The session may include discussion or presentation of potential audiences, platforms, narrative directions, brand-integration opportunities, story worlds, premises, formats, titles, characters, relationships, themes, hooks, episode ideas, and potential production pathways.

Unless Palava expressly agrees otherwise in writing, the session does not include:

  • A recording or transcript
  • A take-home presentation or session deck
  • A written concept or custom creative brief
  • Scripts, treatments, outlines, series bibles, or episode arcs
  • Production services
  • A transfer or license of any creative rights

Client may invite employees or representatives whose attendance has been approved by Palava. Client is responsible for ensuring that every attendee complies with these terms.

2. No Recording or Automated Transcription

Client and all attendees may not record, photograph, screen-capture, transcribe, reproduce, or use automated note-taking, transcription, or artificial-intelligence tools during the session without Palava’s prior written consent.

Palava may suspend or end the session if unauthorized recording or transcription occurs.

3. Palava Materials

“Palava Materials” means all concepts, premises, titles, characters, relationships, settings, story worlds, formats, narrative systems, hooks, episode ideas, dialogue, written material, visual material, presentation material, analysis, strategy, recommendations, and other creative or intellectual material that:

  1. Palava created before the session;
  2. Palava presents during the session; or
  3. Palava creates, proposes, articulates, or develops during the session, whether independently or through discussion with Client.

Palava retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Palava Materials, including all applicable intellectual-property rights.

Payment for or participation in the session does not constitute a sale, assignment, work-made-for-hire arrangement, implied license, option, shopping agreement, production authorization, or other transfer of rights.

4. No License or Authorized Use

Unless Palava gives prior written authorization or the parties enter into a separate written development, licensing, or production agreement, Client may not directly or indirectly:

  • Use or exploit any Palava Materials;
  • Develop, produce, publish, advertise, distribute, or commission content based on any Palava Materials;
  • Adapt, revise, combine, imitate, or create derivative material based on any Palava Materials;
  • Provide any Palava Materials to an agency, studio, production company, writer, director, employee, contractor, affiliate, vendor, creator, consultant, or other third party;
  • Solicit bids, estimates, proposals, pitches, scripts, or production services based on any Palava Materials;
  • Use the Palava Materials as the basis for an internal creative brief, request for proposal, campaign, or production assignment.

Any permitted development, production, licensing, or use must be stated in a separate written agreement signed by Client and Palava.

5. Confidentiality

Client will keep the nonpublic creative contents of the session confidential and use them solely to evaluate whether to engage Palava for further development or production.

Client may disclose the existence and general business purpose of the session internally, including that the parties discussed audience strategy, narrative direction, brand integration, and potential production.

Client may not disclose specific Palava Materials to anyone who did not attend the session without Palava’s prior written permission.

Each person attending on Client’s behalf must be informed of and comply with these terms. Client remains responsible for any breach by its employees, representatives, agencies, contractors, affiliates, invitees, or other persons to whom Client provides access.

6. Client Materials

Client retains ownership of its preexisting names, trademarks, logos, products, campaigns, proprietary data, and other materials supplied to Palava.

Client grants Palava a limited right to review and discuss those materials solely to prepare for and conduct the Creative Session.

Nothing in these terms gives Palava ownership of Client’s preexisting brand assets.

7. Session Fee and Production Credit

The Creative Session fee is $1,500, payable at booking.

The fee is subject to the cancellation, refund, and rescheduling conditions presented during booking.

If Client enters into a qualifying Pilot production agreement with Palava within the period specified by Palava, the $1,500 Creative Session fee will be credited against the amount payable under that agreement.

The credit:

  • Has no cash value;
  • Is nontransferable;
  • Does not itself grant any production or creative rights; and
  • Does not obligate Palava to accept or begin a later engagement.

8. No Obligation to Proceed

Neither Client nor Palava is required to enter into a subsequent development, production, licensing, or other agreement.

Palava is not obligated to reserve availability, begin development, provide additional materials, or perform further creative work unless the parties sign a separate written agreement and Client pays any required deposit.

9. Remedies

Client acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Palava Materials may cause harm that is difficult to measure solely through monetary damages.

Palava may pursue any remedies available under the parties’ agreement or applicable law, including appropriate injunctive relief and monetary damages.

10. Authority and Electronic Acceptance

The person accepting these terms represents that they:

  • Are at least eighteen years old;
  • Have authority to accept these terms; and
  • If booking for a company or other organization, have authority to bind that Client to these terms.

By checking the required acceptance box during booking and completing the booking, Client agrees to these terms electronically and intends that action to serve as Client’s acceptance.

11. Entire Agreement for the Creative Session

These Creative Session Terms, together with the booking, payment, cancellation, and rescheduling conditions displayed during checkout, constitute the agreement governing the Creative Session.

Any amendment, license, transfer, waiver, or permission concerning Palava Materials must be in writing and expressly accepted by Palava.
